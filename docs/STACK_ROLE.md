# Stack Role: cc-framework

cc-framework is the scientific kernel of the Ghost Protocol ecosystem.

Its responsibility is not product UI, branding, dashboards, or enterprise workflow. Its responsibility is the measurable core:

- dependence-aware guardrail composition
- Fréchet-Hoeffding bounds
- composition coefficient computation
- regime classification
- deterministic evidence bundles
- manifests, hashes, ledgers, and attestations
- reproducible experimental outputs

This repository should remain the place where claims become mathematics and mathematics becomes auditable code.

If Ghost Protocol is the civilization, cc-framework is the law of physics.
