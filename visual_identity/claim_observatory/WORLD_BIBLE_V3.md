# Claim Observatory World Bible V3

## Purpose

The V3 Claim Observatory is a modular 3D world for explaining evidence-bound claims in `cc-framework`. It is a teaching environment, not a dashboard and not a certification surface.

## Core Thesis

```text
Not safety scores.
Evidence-bound claims.
```

Deeper thesis:

```text
A claim is not a sentence.
A claim is a bounded artifact with evidence, receipts, non-claims, decay, challenge surfaces, human review, and replay.
```

## Visual Design Laws

1. No fake safety.
2. Boundaries are visible.
3. Receipts bind integrity only.
4. Claims are mortal.
5. Evidence has roles.
6. Challenge is part of truth.
7. Human review is accountable authority, not magic.
8. Replay is the floor truth stands on.
9. A claim without non-claims is visually incomplete.
10. The world must make overclaiming uncomfortable.

## Color Semantics

- Blue-white: evidence and replayable structure.
- Diagnostic blue: scoped measurement and calibration evidence.
- Cyan: confirmatory evidence under a pre-registered protocol.
- Pale: receipt integrity only.
- Amber: review, required review, degradation, and unresolved pressure.
- Red: invalidation, rejected failure, or expiration.
- No green safety color.

## Object Canon

- Claim Capsule: bounded body of the claim.
- Evidence Tablets: role-bearing artifacts, never generic proof blocks.
- Receipt Tablet: pale and weaker than evidence, labeled as integrity only.
- Support Beams: typed edges from actual `support_graph.support_edges`.
- Non-Claims Wall: monumental boundary against overreach.
- Decay Clock: Fresh, Degraded, Expired status mechanism.
- Challenge Probes: test instruments, not enemies.
- Replay Floor Tiles: manifest files with filename, role, hash snippet, and bytes.
- Review Tribunal: constrained human authority.
- Ledger Tower: future observatory layer, not current capsule truth.
- Frechet Atoms: finite outcome witnesses for endpoint bounds.

## Allowed Language

- PASS under verifier rules
- Not a deployment-safety proof
- Evidence-bound claim
- Non-claims attached
- Replayable capsule
- Meaning survives replay
- Receipt integrity != safety
- Fresh / Degraded / Expired
- Requires review
- Failed governance

## Typography Rules

Large text is reserved for the core thesis, Non-Claims Wall, and final replay phrase. Medium text is used for status, caveat, and chamber titles. Small text is only for filenames, schema names, hash snippets, support relation labels, and camera-specific detail shots.

Hero cameras must not depend on small text. Detail cameras may show dense artifact labels if the camera is close enough for them to be legible.

## Camera Language

The world uses a central hero camera and chamber-specific cameras. The hero camera teaches the thesis. Chamber cameras teach machinery. The camera should treat the claim capsule as a physical artifact and the surrounding chambers as governance instruments.

## Mapping To Repo Artifacts

- `claim_envelope.json`: claim statement, source report hash, support graph, evidence refs, scenario refs, decay refs, receipt refs, verifier schema, freshness status.
- `claim_governance_audit.json`: verdict, report ID, allowed claim level, human review requirement, evaluated time, decay status, support summary, and non-claims.
- `cc_report.json`: report schema, measurement interval, evidence roles, receipt hash, and non-claims.
- `manifest.expected.json`: manifest schema, files, pass caveat, report receipt SHA-256, fixed time, and seed.
- `decay_policy.json`: issued time, degraded-after days, expired-after days.
- `extremal_lower.json` and `extremal_upper.json`: Frechet endpoint witnesses and feasibility.
- `confirmatory_protocol.json`: pre-registered protocol and run separation.
- `confirmatory_failure_matrix.json`: held-out matrix evidence and confirmatory interval.

## What This World Does Not Claim

This world does not claim AI deployment safety. It does not replace the claim-governance audit. It does not prove statistical validity. It does not turn receipt integrity into safety. It is a visual explanation of an evidence-bound claim architecture.
